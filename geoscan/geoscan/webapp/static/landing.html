◆ OreSight

Copper prospectivity mapping from satellite data

OreSight maps the surface alteration that copper-bearing fluids leave behind, traces the structures those fluids may have used, and ranks prospective zones for field follow-up.

  • Alteration minerals (muscovite, chlorite, gossan, quartz) from NASA EMIT and ASTER imagery
  • Lineaments from a 30 m elevation model, combined into a Copper Prospectivity Index
  • Ranked zones on an interactive map, with adjustable parameters and downloads for GIS
